The feature presentation

Rush fanatics, Dreamer and Misfit, venture into the enigmatic halls of Xanarena in search of the greatest rock show ever. Guided by two cryptic crows and a stolen map, they must navigate through haunted halls with the looming threat of Working Man's curse. Armed with nothing but a guitar pick, a sustainable bag, and fueled by Alex Lifeson's coffee, Dreamer and Misfit must brave the horrors within to find Rush before they exit stage left...or is it house left?

Color / Approx. 5 minutes / Unrated
Languages / Closed Captions: English, Canadian, Troll
