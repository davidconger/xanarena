Live in 1, 1B, 1C

The power light blinks YYZ

POWER
-.--Y -.--Y --..Z

The reference: YYZ is the airport code for Toronto Pearson. The song "YYZ" from Moving Pictures (1981) opens with that rhythm in Morse code.

Where: the deck's power light loops it quietly. Nobody has to notice; the ones who do will grin.

Live in 1, 1B, 1C

The tape counter stops at 21:12

COUNTER0:00:00

The reference: 2112 (1976) is the album that saved their career. On a VCR it's just a number, so only fans will see it.

Where: the LCD on the deck. It rolls to 0:21:12 on boot, stop and fast forward.

The references: Pause says "Time stand still" (Hold Your Fire, 1987). FF says "Fly by night" (Fly by Night, 1975). Bad tracking shows "Down the tubes tour," the band's own nickname for the Caress of Steel tour, when the album sold poorly and they played small rooms.

CC: cycles English, Canadian, Troll and "Blah blah blah," after Alex's 2013 Rock and Roll Hall of Fame speech, which he delivered as nothing but "blah blah blah." Rewind stays "Rewind," because "Be kind, rewind" carries the concept.

Live in 1C

Distant early warning

Distant early warning This videocassette is licensed for private home viewing by Rush fans only. Unauthorized exits, stage left, house left or otherwise, are strictly prohibited.

The reference: a song from Grace Under Pressure (1984), the same year as the concept 1 basement. The title comes from the DEW Line, a chain of Cold War radar stations across the Canadian Arctic. A Canadian reference, used as a warning screen.

Live in 1, 1B, 1C

Secret codes

Try: YYZ sends the Morse beeps through the power light. 2112 rolls the counter and puts up "Overture. The temples have been notified." Overture is the first section of the 2112 suite, and the Temples of Syrinx are the priests who run that story's world.

Rule: a generated tone only, never the song's audio.

MockFor 1B

Three more things in the 1B night scene

  • An owl on the wire
  • A dalmatian at the hydrant
  • A stenciled guitar case

The owl: Fly by Night (1975) has an owl on the cover, and the scene is already at night. Its eyes blink out of step with the crows'.

The dalmatian: a dog sniffing a fire hydrant is the cover of Signals (1982). In the scene it's just a dog on a street, and fans will recognize it.

The guitar case: in the 2112 story, a man finds a forgotten guitar and the Priests of the Temples of Syrinx smash it, because they control all music. In 1B the case gets dropped and something gets out, a fitting comeback for that guitar.

MockNeeds sign-off

Anika's laminate

ALL ACCESS
Anika Nilles
Die Schlagzeugerin
Xanarena · Climate Pledge Arena

The reference: Neil's drum solos were named "The Drummer" in other languages: "O Baterista" on Rush in Rio, "Der Trommler" on R30, "De Slagwerker" on Snakes & Arrows Live. Anika is German, and Schlagzeugerin is the feminine form. It welcomes her by carrying on his tradition.

Ask first: it ties her to Neil's legacy, so the band's team should say yes.
