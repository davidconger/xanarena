Flyers

Six 8.5 x 11 flyers: three portrait posters and three quieter landscape riffs. All are built to print on a regular office printer. Open one and print with paper size Letter and margins None. Set the real site address in flyers/flyer-config.js and every QR code updates.

1. Be Kind, Rewind Video store poster. The QR code is the tape's handwritten rental label. 2. Viewer Beware Kids-horror slime title, crows on a chain-link fence, and a "Scan if you dare" sign. 3. House Left Show handbill with a will-call ticket and eight tear-off tickets, each with its own QR.

Subtle, landscape

One idea per page, turned sideways. Same print settings: Letter, margins None. The browser picks landscape on its own.

1B. The Tape Just a VHS cassette. The QR code lives on the center label. 2B. The Fence Crows on a chain-link fence at night, with the QR on a sign wired to the posts. 3B. The Ticket One big will-call ticket. "Scan if you dare" sits above the QR.
